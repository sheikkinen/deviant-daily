# Hunger in the Neon Abyss

She crawls through a cathedral of light—neon runes burning crimson and cyan beneath her clawed hands, each symbol pulsing like a living heartbeat. Her face is a mask of feral delight, skin dark and contoured by the glow, eyes blazing orange with an intelligence that cuts through the chaos of color. Curved horns rise from her crown, catching the electric light in sharp relief, while her grin widens with a hunger that the runes themselves seem to answer.

The floor beneath her is alive with sigils—circles and spirals that bleed luminescence across the obsidian surface, casting her form in alternating washes of heat and cold. Her muscular frame leans forward with predatory grace, arms extended as though claiming dominion over the arcane geometry spread before her. The tribal marking visible on her chest glows faintly, a second heartbeat to the runes that surround her, binding flesh to sorcery.

Behind her, the world dissolves into shadow and distant flame, suggesting depths both infernal and infinite. The neon light creates halos of pink, gold, and electric blue—a color symphony that transforms the scene into something between a summoning circle and a throne room. She is both supplicant and sovereign, both creature and conjurer, suspended in a moment where power and hunger become indistinguishable.

In this realm where sigil-light and shadow dance, she is the center around which all magic turns. Her expression holds no apology, no restraint—only the raw certainty of something that has clawed its way through dimensions to claim what it desires. Be Art. Be Unique.
