# Sigils Breaking Open the Sky

A storm of ink-black smoke coils around a young figure whose head is thrown back in laughter, dark wind-tossed hair streaming against a pale, blazing light near the top of the frame. Molten veins of red, orange, and gold crawl across the shoulders, arms, and hands, cracking like cooling lava over skin that seems to hold the heat within it.

Around them the air is thick with spiraling glyphs, thin white and gold loops, and rune-marked curls that drift like sparks escaping a forge. Lightning-bright seams of teal and amber run down the left side of the canvas, pooling into scorched, lava-veined rock where the storm has torn the ground open.

The hands reach inward, cupping a knot of burning sigils as though gathering the surge before it can scatter. Nothing about the scene looks calm; the apprentice seems caught between mastery and mischief, the grin wide even as the arcane weather tears at their body and the world around them.

Whatever bargain was struck here, the storm has not yet been tamed. It keeps spinning, keeps glowing, and keeps asking who will be consumed first. Be Art. Be Unique.

> "Hold still. The fire wants to sing, and I want to hear it."
