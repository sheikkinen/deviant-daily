# Salt Wind and Red Cord

A woman with long dreadlocks stands bound to a weathered wooden mast on a sun-bleached pirate ship deck, her arms lashed overhead with red rope and her body wrapped in the same crimson lines. The planks run toward a bright blue horizon where white surf breaks against the hull.

Sunlight falls hard across the deck, throwing a long, angular shadow over the boards and sharpening every knot and fiber of the rope. Cream lace sleeves puff at her shoulders, and a black lace bodice sits beneath the red lacing, so the costume feels like a sea-worn relic caught in a moment of defiance.

Her face is set in a fierce, unyielding stare, lips dark and brows drawn, as if she refuses to give the crew the satisfaction of fear. The rigging lines that frame the frame and the open water beyond suggest a ship under way, with no witness but the wind.

The scene holds its tension between captivity and composure, a story left unfinished on the open sea. Be Art. Be Unique.
