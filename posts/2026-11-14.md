# Sparks Dimmed in the Repair Bay

A cramped android repair shop hums under a flickering tube light, its pipes and gray tanks sweating in the cold. Consoles glow with pale schematic lines, and cables spill across the concrete like dark roots.

At the center, a pale android sits with her knees drawn up, exposed joints and blue, orange, and red wires running along her neck, arms, and torso. Her tank-top is torn open, and tears track down her cheeks in thin silver lines while her lips part in a quiet, broken expression.

Beside her, a figure in a cap and worn work jacket crouches close, one gloved hand resting on her shoulder. Tools hang from a belt near a rack of pliers, and a small metal part lies forgotten on the floor, suggesting a repair interrupted by grief.

The room holds its breath between hum and silence. Be Art. Be Unique.

> "Just a little longer... the sparks haven't gone out yet."
