# Ember Vigil at the Undying Anvil

Smoke coils upward from a dim forge hall, lifting slowly past the rafters while sparks break loose from the anvil and scatter into the dark. Two hearths burn behind him, one on the left and one on the right, throwing a warm amber glow across timber walls and the scattered debris of an old working floor.

At the center stands a gaunt, long-haired smith in a weathered leather coat and armored forearm guards. His face is drawn into a snarl, teeth bared and eyes set hard on the blade. A small round medallion hangs at his chest, catching the firelight like a dull relic. He leans into each blow with the whole of his body, one hand gripping a heavy hammer, the other bracing the glowing steel.

The blade lies across the anvil, its edge bright orange where the metal yields under the strike. Each impact throws a fan of sparks that hangs in the air like a brief constellation. Nothing here is calm; the forge feels like a place where labor has become a kind of penance, repeated until the night itself gives way.
