# Tide Light Over the Dry Ridge

A woman turns her gaze upward, her pale face crazed with fine cracks like a glaze left too long in the sun. Above her brow, a translucent dome pulses with cyan light, its tendrils drifting down past her cheeks and shoulders as though the sea had risen to meet her in the dust.

Brass goggles sit across her forehead, their lenses clouded with reflected glow. Behind her ear, sealed teal lamps glow against dark cabling and worn gears, small beacons of a machine that still remembers the tides. Iridescent scales catch violet and seafoam light along her jaw and neck, where the organic and the engineered seem to grow into one another.

Behind her, sandstone spires rise out of a hazy amber desert, the sky warm with late light. The contrast is quiet but deliberate: a creature built for deep water standing in a landscape where water has long since gone. Her expression is steady and searching, as if she is watching for something on the horizon that only she can see.

She keeps watch over the dry ridges, carrying the last bioluminescent tide inside her. Whatever she is waiting for, the glow at her temple suggests it will arrive in its own time. Be Art. Be Unique.

> "The tide is still in me. Wait for it."
