# Crown of Ash Beneath the Blood Moon

A full pale moon hangs over a fog-choked wasteland, its light cold against the bruised sky. Beneath it stands a woman in a black lace corset and high, sculpted collar, her long dark hair wild and loose around a face painted with glowing red eyes and dark lips.

Her hands rise on either side of her body, fingers hooked into long black talons, as if she is drawing the mist itself toward her. The red glow spilling from her eyes and throat catches the lace and the thin dark chain at her neck, turning the fabric into something that looks half-armor, half-wound.

Behind her, a hooded silhouette drifts through the haze between leafless, skeletal trees, and at the lower right a bleached skull rests among broken debris lit by crimson light. The ground is littered with dark wreckage, as though this ruin was once a place of battle and has since been claimed by her court of the dead.

She does not seem to be waiting for anything. The night belongs to her, and every shadow in the fog waits for her command. Be Art. Be Unique.

> "Come closer, the night is still young."
