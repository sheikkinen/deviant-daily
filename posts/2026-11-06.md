# Chart of the Veiled Odyssey

An antique sea chart unfolds across weathered parchment, its edges cracked and darkened as if it has been pulled from a sunken chest. A dashed route winds through the middle, looping past a towering waterfall that pours from a crumbling cliff-city into a turquoise basin, marked as the Falls of Aeldrin, where the veil grows thin.

On the left, a dark-haired woman in a shadowed cloak and gold chain ornaments turns her profile toward the sea, her earrings and headpiece catching the faint light. Beside her, small ships sail toward the Siren's Reef, and a gilded compass rose rests on the paper like a relic of old navigators.

On the right, a hooded skeletal figure with a scythe rises from swirling grey mist, the Specter of Captain Vorne, who sails still though the sea took him. Below him, an open treasure chest spills gold coins beside a skull, marked with a bold X and labeled as the Treasure of the Forgotten King, cursed be the greedy hand.

Above the falls, a golden star burst and concentric rings glow against the night-blue sky, drawing the route upward like a guiding sun. The Stars guide. The dead remember. The brave continue. Be Art. Be Unique.

> "Many have heard, few have returned."
