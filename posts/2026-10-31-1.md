# Vines Climbing the Troll's Crown

A winged warrior stands astride a moss-green troll whose heavy brow and open, fanged jaws loom over a rocky ledge. Her feathered wings sweep up behind her in pale silver and lavender, and her curls spill in wild golden waves around a face caught mid-shout, eyes narrowed in fierce triumph.

Behind the scene, a great luminous ring glows through a veil of pale mist, its amber spokes and thin gold lines suggesting an armillary or orrery turning in some celestial hall. The light is soft and cool at the edges, warm where it falls across her arms and the bright blade.

Her sword is driven into the troll's skull, its gilt crossguard catching the light beside her gloved grip. Around the stone beneath them, fungal caps in coral and cream bloom among creeping green vines, and a few tendrils wind up along her bare thigh beneath her riveted chainmail skirt. The troll's brow is furrowed in pain or defeat, and the whole tableau feels like a battle just won at the edge of a strange sky. Be Art. Be Unique.
