# Where the Orchard Keeps Its Whispers

Twisted apple trees stand in a long, fog-choked avenue, their gnarled limbs knotting against a pale moon that glows faintly behind the bare canopy. Some branches still hang with small red fruit, a few swaying above the ground like lanterns no one came back to light.

Mist pools between the trunks and curls low over dark, churned earth. Fallen apples lie scattered across the path, some green, some red, many bruised and sunk into the mud, as if the harvest was abandoned mid-season and the orchard has been slowly giving its fruit back to the soil.

Two pale, hooded silhouettes drift in the middle distance, half-swallowed by the haze. They seem to belong to the orchard rather than to the living world, lingering at the edge of the path as though waiting for someone to pass beneath the branches.

At the right edge, a skeletal hand, pale and long-fingered, grips the bark of a trunk as if the tree itself were reaching back. The orchard keeps its secrets in silence, and anyone who walks its rows will hear what the wind leaves behind. Be Art. Be Unique.

> "Pick the fruit, and the orchard will remember your name."
