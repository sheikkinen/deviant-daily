# Proboscis Over the Sleeping Balcony

A winged thing hangs in the dark air between carved stone galleries, its bat-like wings spread wide enough to block the upper arches. Its body is long and ribbed, its head narrowed into a faceless, segmented proboscis that trails down toward the ledge below.

Below, a figure lies stretched along a stone balcony, pale clothing catching the warm light that spills from a doorway at the left. The sleeper does not stir. Carved reliefs and weathered columns frame the scene, and the city around them seems to hold its breath.

Warm amber light bleeds from behind a column in the lower left, pushing the rest of the scene into deep teal and black. The creature's clawed feet are tucked beneath it, each talon curved like a hooked blade, and the stone around it shows the cracked patience of a city that has grown old in its own decadence. Long shadows stretch across the masonry.

The night is quiet and the architecture is ornate, but the descent is already underway. Whatever the creature intends, the balcony is the only place the light does not reach fully. Be Art. Be Unique.
