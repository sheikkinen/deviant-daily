# Lace of Cinder and Bloom

A figure stands at the heart of a dim cavern, her pale form rising from a floor of wet stone and shallow water. Above her, a cold shaft of light falls from somewhere beyond the frame, catching fine particles that drift like ash through the mist.

Her wings are not feathered but woven, a lattice of translucent veins that branches outward in long, torn spans. Where the light touches them they glow silver-white, and where they fray at the edges they seem to dissolve into the dark, as if the wings were made of breath and old spider silk.

Her gown grows out of the earth itself. Clusters of coral-pink and ivory blossoms gather at the bodice and hips, while below them the skirt becomes a porous, root-like mass of rust and charcoal that spreads across the ground and pools into the water, binding her to the cave.

She gazes upward with parted lips, unafraid of the dark around her. The story seems to be one of a guardian who blooms from decay, keeping a quiet vigil where light enters the underground. Be Art. Be Unique.
