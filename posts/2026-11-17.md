# Thorned Hymn Beneath the Crimson Roots

Seen from directly above, a horned creature lies sprawled across a bed of tangled crimson growth, its limbs flung outward as if it has fallen through the roof of some underground chapel. Its skin is dark bronze and wet-looking, streaked with lighter veins that catch the light like tarnished metal, and its long fingers end in curved black claws that reach toward the edges of the frame.

The vines around it are not still. Red filaments thread through the blackness in glittering strands, each one dusted with small points of light that suggest frost, embers, or sediment drifting in still air. Between them the darkness opens in pockets, and the whole scene feels suspended between growth and decay, as though the roots have been feeding on whatever lies beneath.

Two great ivory horns curl out from the creature's head, ridged and sharply tipped, framing a face half-hidden in shadow. A wing membrane, dark and veined with red, spreads behind the shoulders. Near the bottom edge, a tail coils in a pale, engraved spiral, its filigree catching a thin strip of warm light against the dense black.

The stillness is heavy, the kind that comes before a sermon or a summons. Nothing here moves fast; everything seems to be waiting for the moment the sleeper opens his eyes. Be Art. Be Unique.

> "The roots remember every name you have forgotten."
