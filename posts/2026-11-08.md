# Heartmoon Over the Green Current

A heart-shaped moon hangs pale above a dense, emerald canopy, its glow filtering through broad leaves and drifting across a slow blue-green stream that winds out of sight at the lower left. The air reads as humid and still, the kind of stillness that comes just before something stirs in the undergrowth.

A woman sits astride a thick, moss-spotted branch, one arm hooked around the trunk and a long spear held upright in her other hand. Her black hair falls past her shoulders, and her dark skin takes on warm bronze highlights against the saturated greens. Studded leather bands wrap her wrists and upper arm, and a beaded belt with fringed leather cords sits low on her hips.

Her gaze is direct and unhurried, carrying the composure of someone who has claimed this ground and does not need to prove it. Small dark ornaments mark the details of her body, and her posture is open and defiant, her knee raised as she surveys whoever approaches along the river path.

The comic-book inking, with its hard black contours and flat, bold colour blocks, gives the jungle the feel of a page torn from an old adventure serial. Be Art. Be Unique.

> "The river knows my name, and so will you."
