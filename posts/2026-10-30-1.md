# Storm Over the Burning Fleet

Rain slants across a bruised sky where smoke columns rise from two blazing galleons, their rigging silhouetted against orange fire. A storm-battered coast of wet rock and tufted grass frames the scene, and drifting debris hangs in the air like the last breath of a naval engagement.

At the center, a red-haired captain in a black tricorn and a cracked red leather corset lunges forward, long hair streaming sideways in the wind. Her arms are extended with a curved cutlass in one hand and a second blade in the other, while torn sleeves and a ragged skirt snap around her legs as if the gale itself is pulling at her.

Low on the grass, a kneeling figure with a cow's head and a red polka-dot bandana watches the fight in stillness. The contrast between her fierce motion and the quiet, bare-shouldered figure beside her gives the battlefield an odd, intimate tension, as though the sea war has spilled onto a meadow where no one else is left to stand.
