# The First Taste, Woven in Green Light

A woman with auburn hair spilling in long Art Nouveau waves bites into a golden apple, its surface etched with fine scrollwork. Where her lips meet the fruit, a small pink-and-cyan glitch blooms, and pale teal filaments pour from her mouth like threads of light drawn out across the air.

Behind her the garden is already half dissolving. On the left, lilies, lotus blossoms, and two peacocks rest in a dense bower of gold-edged foliage, still and untroubled. On the right, the ground turns to rows of binary code and rising bar charts, and a green serpent with glowing eyes coils around a silver branch, its body banded like cable.

Faint circuit-like sprays of light rise from her temple, sketching leaf shapes in the hair that seem to answer the thought moving behind her gaze. Her eyes are wide and fixed on something beyond the frame, and the mood is hushed, suspended between wonder and loss.

Consciousness expands across the arch above her, and the title band below names the moment plainly. Green and gold hold the whole scene together, so the taste of knowledge arrives as both a garden's ending and a door swinging open. Be Art. Be Unique.
