# Moonlit Lunar Duel

A full moon hangs over a pale, cratered plain, its cold light cutting through a sky of near-black ink. Across the dusty ground, a hooded figure in dark layered wrappings crouches low, one arm flung forward, a slender blade angled across the frame like a line drawn in a single stroke.

The hood frames a sharp, unblinking face, and bandaged wrists hold a short weapon ready at the shoulder. Behind the ninja, a pale-haired woman in a loose, torn dress lunges across the scene, mouth open, hair streaming in a wind that does not seem to touch the ground. Her outstretched hand reaches toward the hooded guard as if the chase has only just begun.

The palette is strictly monochrome: bone-white highlights, heavy black shadows, and scratched textures in the soil and sky. Every edge reads like woodcut or ink, giving the duel a stylized, printed quality that feels older than the moon itself.
