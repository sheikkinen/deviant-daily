# Feast of Horns and Halos

A candlelit hall hums beneath a gilded chandelier, its brass arms holding a crown of pale flames over a long table draped in white linen. Plates of roasted meats, bright fruit, cheeses, and golden pastries crowd the cloth, and deep red wine glints in stemmed glasses held by every guest.

At the center sits a dark-haired figure with curling black horns, a black corset edged in lace, and bat-like wings folded behind her. One leg is stretched across the table in a relaxed, unhurried pose, and she lifts a thin pale stick to her lips with a slow, knowing air. Her eyes are half-closed, as if the feast has taken her somewhere private.

Around her the gathering shifts between courtly and wild. A blue-scaled dragon with curved horns looms at the left edge, jaws parted in a rough grin. A fair-haired woman in white, with feathered wings rising behind her shoulders, tips a glass back with her eyes closed, while a copper-haired guest in violet and a woman in red satin raise their cups in open delight.

Violet and magenta light spills through the arched windows at the back of the hall, softening the gold trim and the crimson drapery. The mood is indulgent and theatrical, a celebration where myth and appetite share the same table. Be Art. Be Unique.
