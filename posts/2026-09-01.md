# Lich Lord's Whiskey Reckoning

A skeletal sorcerer crowned in shadow raises a glass of amber whiskey against a war-torn sky, blue arcane energy coiling from ornate shoulder pauldrons. The lich's hollow eyes glow with eldritch light as it stands amid the chaos of battle, commanding magic that tears through the heavens themselves. Behind the figure, a twisted staff bristles with dark iron thorns, crackling with the same cyan power that radiates from the creature's armored form.

The landscape burns in shades of rust and flame—distant armies clash in silhouette against an orange-brown haze, their weapons raised like thorns across the horizon. Dust and embers drift through the air, each particle catching the light of distant fires and the supernatural glow emanating from the lich's body. The chiaroscuro is merciless: deep shadow clings to the creature's robes while highlights define every edge of its armor and the whiskey glass held aloft.

This is a moment of terrible triumph—a being of death and sorcery pausing amid carnage to savor its power, as if the very act of drinking is an assertion of dominion. The dynamic composition pulls the eye upward and outward, following the arc of the staff and the explosion of magical force. Every detail speaks of a world where war and magic are indistinguishable, where ancient evil wears the face of victory.

The artwork captures a dark apotheosis: the convergence of battle-lust, necromantic power, and a defiant gesture against mortality itself.
