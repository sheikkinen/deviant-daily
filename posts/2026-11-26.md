# Ember Eyes at the Waterline

A red fox rises from still water at the hour the sun sinks behind a low line of dark trees. Its ears are tall and tipped in black, and its amber eyes hold steady on the viewer while the surface breaks in slow, widening rings around its shoulders.

The lagoon looks worn at its edges. Dark flecks of debris and clumps of green algae cling to the fox's wet shoulders and drift across the ripples, and the water gleams with reflected orange light where the sun has dropped to a pale disc above the haze.

Mist lifts faintly from the far shore, softening the silhouettes of the trees into a grey-violet band. Wet strands of reddish fur hang close against the neck and cheeks, giving the figure a damp, weathered look, as though it has only just come up out of the murk.

The scene holds a quiet, watchful stillness, with nothing moving but the water and the glow of the evening. Be Art. Be Unique.

> "The water remembers everyone who wades in at dusk."
