# Hunger Wears a Thousand Eyes

The transformation is complete and still unfolding. What was human now writhes beneath a carapace of liquid shadow—tendrils of opalescent darkness writhe across skin like arterial flows, each tendril a hunger given form. The symbiote's presence is absolute: bioluminescent tracery in viridian and cyan maps the invasion across torso and shoulders, marking the pathways of alien consciousness consuming the host from within.

The eyes betray the horror most plainly. Not one pair but many—luminous orbs of sickly green, each burning with predatory intelligence, each seeing, each hungering. They pierce through the black membrane like lanterns in an abyss, their gaze multidirectional and alien. Between them, where a human mouth once drew breath, fangs bare themselves in a rictus that might be agony or ecstasy; the distinction has become meaningless.

Tendrils coil and dance in the still air, their tips tapering to needlepoints, their surfaces slick with a substance neither liquid nor solid. Cyan lightning traces the symbiote's neural pathways in stark contrast to the oily darkness. The dramatic sidelight casts impossible shadows—shadows that move against the source, shadows that seem to have their own geometry, their own will.

In this moment between human and other, between individual and collective hunger, something ancient and patient smiles. The host's hands still grasp at their own transformation, fingers splayed in a gesture lost between resistance and surrender. This is the price of symbiosis: the dissolution of self into something vast, something eternal, something ravenous. Be Art. Be Unique.

> We are many. We are hunger. We are becoming.
