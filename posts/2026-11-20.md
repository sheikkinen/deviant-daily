# Emerald Queen in the Sulphur Haze

A green-skinned demon queen reclines against a rising wall of sulphur steam, her head tipped back and eyes closed as pale smoke coils around her in slow, gold-tinted ribbons. Ornate gilt crowns of jeweled points frame her face, and small red stones glint along her collar, bracelets, and a chain that drapes across her body.

Her body is marked with dark, vein-like tracings, and the black, cracked armour or hide clinging to her hip and thigh has split open at the edges. Behind her, the rock faces lean inward, and the air glows yellow where drifting vapour catches a light source below the frame.

Glowing glyphs hover over the scene like embers: angular runes drift near her arm and along the shadowed ground, marking the space as a place where old pacts are kept and spoken. The smoke seems to thicken whenever she breathes, as if the caverns themselves are answering her.

She is a ruler who does not need to rise to be obeyed; the vapours rise for her instead. Be Art. Be Unique.
