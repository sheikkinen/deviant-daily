# Banners of Valor Above the Void

Within a vast, gilded bridge curved like the hull of an ancient galleon, a woman with a sculpted dark bun stands in a black cape edged with gold filigree, her back to us and her gaze fixed on the window. Beyond the glass, the stars bloom in deep blue nebula clouds, and a colossal armored war-beast with glowing red eyes rears against a field of drifting fleets.

Red beams stitch across the blackness, cutting between the spiked silhouettes of cybernetic warships and the distant glimmer of a fortified city-platform. The crew moves through a blue-lit ring of consoles: a seated figure with a head of dark hair, a scaled green alien leaning over a holographic tactical table, and tall, cloaked officers pacing the deck.

Two luminous banners hang from the curved wall. The violet one carries the word VALOR and the line HONOR IS OUR SHIELD, while the teal one reads LEGACY and WE ARE THE STARS REMEMBER. Their glowing script gives the chamber the feeling of a shrine to old oaths, as though every decision made here will be recorded in the constellations.

The mood is calm before a storm: no shots have landed yet, only the tension of commanders who have already chosen their course. Warm gold light meets cold blue glow, and the bridge seems to hold its breath while the fleets close in across the dark, honoring the vows written on its walls. Be Art. Be Unique.

> We are the stars remember.
