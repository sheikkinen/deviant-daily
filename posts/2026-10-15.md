# Digital Damnation

In a vaulted chamber where baroque grandeur meets the cold glow of cryptocurrency, a horned figure sits enthroned in crimson and gold—a devil reborn in the age of blockchain. Cryptocurrency medallions dangle from his neck like a modern rosary, each coin a promise and a curse. In his hand, a glowing tablet displays NFT galleries and trading charts, the instruments of his dominion. Around him, the floor is scattered with golden coins and holographic blockchain symbols that cast their ethereal light across the marble.

Two figures stand trapped behind ornate golden cages, their hands pressed against screens that glow like prison bars. They gesture in desperation toward the central figure, their forms rendered in muted earth tones—prisoners of a digital vault they helped construct. The cages themselves are framed like classical artworks, a cruel irony: beauty as confinement, wealth as bondage.

Floating above and around the throne, pixelated NFT galleries hang in gilded frames—colorful digital artifacts rendered as fine art, a mockery of creation itself. Blockchain visualizations form geometric patterns in cyan and green light, replacing the old symbols of greed with new ones. The architecture towers with neoclassical columns, yet every surface hums with data streams and algorithmic hunger.

The scene breathes with spiritual emptiness—opulent yet hollow, a masterwork of classical technique devoted to the worship of nothing. The devil's expression carries a smug satisfaction, as if he alone understands the joke: that we have built our own cages and called them opportunity. Be Art. Be Unique.

> I offer not gold, but the dream of gold—and that dream is far more profitable.
