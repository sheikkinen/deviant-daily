# Moonlit Vigil of the Weeping Raven

A pale full moon hangs over a bruised blue sky, its surface softened by swirling brushstrokes that ripple outward like water disturbed by a stone. Beneath it, a distant mountain rises out of misty hills, and the silhouette of a spired ruin crowds the far left edge, as if the land itself is holding its breath.

On a lichen-crusted branch stands a enormous black raven, its glossy plumage catching cold highlights along the curve of its back. Thin trails of moisture run from the corner of one eye down its beak, the only soft detail in an otherwise severe, shadowed form. It watches the figure across the gap with a steady, unblinking patience.

Across the branch, a pale-faced man in a high red collar and a long black coat lifts a curved blade toward the sky, his cape flaring behind him like a torn wing. His stance is braced and wary, a belt buckle gleaming gold at his waist, and his gaze is fixed on the bird rather than the moon.

The scene feels like a vow made between hunter and messenger under a witness of silver light. Be Art. Be Unique.

> "Weep if you must, old bird, but tell me what the moon has seen."
