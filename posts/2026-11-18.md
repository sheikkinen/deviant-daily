# Marble Breathing in Neon Static

A marble head turns its averted gaze toward a city that hums in violet and cyan beyond its shoulder. The curls above its brow are carved deep and tender, yet thin fractures run through them like hairline cracks in old stone, and some of those cracks glow with a faint magenta light that seems to seep from somewhere underneath the rock.

Horizontal bands of pink, yellow, and cyan slice across the scene at the hairline, the cheek, and the throat, as if a broadcast signal has briefly torn through the statue. Where the glitch lines pass, the smooth skin gives way to a shimmer of pixels, and then the marble settles back into its quiet, sculpted calm.

Behind the figure, blurred towers rise in soft bokeh, their windows smeared into warm and cold light. The face is lit with a golden-amber glow along one side while the opposite side takes on electric blue, so the stone seems caught between a sunset that never finished and a night city that never sleeps.

In this place the old gods have not vanished; they have been archived, cracked open, and left to glitch in the neon rain. The figure keeps its patient expression as the static moves through it, waiting for the signal that will decide whether it returns to stone or steps fully into the light. Be Art. Be Unique.

> "I am stone, but the signal still remembers my name."
