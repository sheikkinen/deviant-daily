# Infernal Dawn

In the liminal hour between night and day, a winged figure stretches languidly in her sanctuary above the burning kingdoms. Her apartment—a study in supernatural domesticity—holds the warmth of morning light filtered through gossamer curtains, while beyond the window, hellfire paints the sky in violent oranges and crimsons. Horns catch the golden glow; dark wings fold against her silhouette as she yawns in that most vulnerable, most human of gestures.

The kitchen exhales steam from brewing coffee, its ritual smoke curling into the cozy air. A moka pot sits ready on the stovetop; a mug bearing arcane symbols awaits its fill. Around her, the mundane and the infernal coexist—hanging cookware, potted plants, pinned photographs on the wall. This is no throne room, but a dwelling where the extraordinary has learned to dwell in the everyday.

Visible through the wide window lies her domain: a city of spires and fire, a landscape of damnation rendered beautiful by distance and dawn's strange alchemy. Yet she tends to her plants, arranges her space, and prepares her morning beverage with the same quiet intention as any soul seeking solace in routine. The contradiction is the truth: even those born of flame seek the comfort of home.
