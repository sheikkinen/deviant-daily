# Emerald Hours, Velvet Stillness

A woman rests her chin against a long-fingered hand in a room hung with faded blue-and-brown damask. Her dark hair is pinned in sculpted finger waves and rolled curls at the temple, and her heavy-lidded gaze drifts past the viewer, as if she is waiting for a letter that has not yet arrived.

Behind her a crimson curtain falls in broad, painterly strokes, and at the edge of the frame a cut-glass lamp catches the light like a small frozen moon. The air feels close and still, the kind of late afternoon where dust hangs in the lamplight and nothing is hurried.

Teal satin gathers at her shoulders in ruffled folds, and emerald drop earrings hang beside a matching ring and a bracelet of pale aqua stones set in gold. Her lacquered red lips and smudged silver eyeshadow hold the scene in a quiet, theatrical pause.

She has not yet decided whether to speak. Be Art. Be Unique.

> "Not yet. Let the lamp burn a little longer."
