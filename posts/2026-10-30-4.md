# Where the Falls Remember the Sea

A woman's profile rises out of the cliffside like a carved figurehead, her eyes closed, her hair swept back into the mist of a broad waterfall that pours in white ribbons down the dark rock. Ink hatching gives the stone and spray their weight, and a teardrop earring hangs still against the churning water.

Beyond the falls, a pale sun burns behind a flock of birds scattered across the sky, its rays cut into the ink like old navigation lines. Waves break in heavy white crests against scattered rocks, and a small full-rigged ship sails toward the foreground, carrying the last of the day's light.

On the right, a ghostly galleon hangs above the horizon, its hull ragged and its rigging trailing down through the clouds as if it had drifted out of an older century. Near it, a fortress crowns a rocky island, half lost in the grey haze of the distance.

The sea keeps its secrets in the spray and the shadows, and the wind carries the faint murmur of a story not yet finished. Be Art. Be Unique.
